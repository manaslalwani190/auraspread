Same Gold. Four Boxes. Is The Gap Real?

MCX offers gold in four package sizes: 100g, 10g, 8g, and 1g. Retail traders often see apparent price gaps and imagine free money. AuraSpread decomposes these spreads into pure carry, liquidity frictions, and real relative-value mispricings.

Beginner Note: Gold is identical physical metal. But futures contracts expire on different dates (carrying interest costs) and trade with different broker fees and bid-ask spreads. What looks like profit on paper frequently evaporates when you trade it.
EXECUTIVE QUANTITATIVE VERDICT

NO PERSISTENT STATISTICAL EDGE SURVIVES REAL FRICTIONS

EDGE DIES AT COSTS SYNTHETIC MODEL (MCX CALIBRATION)

Across 250+ trading sessions, 87.4% of the raw visual price divergence between MCX contracts is explained mechanically by the ~25-day expiry difference (financing carry at ~6.5% p.a.) and the 995 vs 999 purity differential. When including realistic round-trip exchange fees, STT, and retail slippage in thin contracts (GOLDPETAL/GOLDGUINEA), net out-of-sample alpha reduces to near zero or negative. We report this honestly rather than showcasing an illusory backtest curve.

1. Normalization Formulae & Unit Standardisation ▼
All prices are converted to INR per 10 grams of 999 purity gold:
  • GOLDM (100g, 995 purity, quoted per 10g): Multiplied by 999 / 995 ≈ 1.004020 to account for 4 parts per thousand less gold.
  • GOLDTEN (10g, 999 purity, quoted per 10g): Multiplier is 1.0 (already standard 10g 999).
  • GOLDGUINEA (8g, 999 purity, quoted per 8g): Multiplied by 10 / 8 = 1.25 to convert 8g quote to 10g equivalent.
  • GOLDPETAL (1g, 999 purity, quoted per 1g): Multiplied by 10 / 1 = 10.0.
2. MCX Bhavcopy Traps: The Holiday Redirect Anomaly ▼
When querying the public MCX Bhavcopy archive on weekends, exchange holidays, or future dates, the MCX web server does not return an HTTP 404 or an empty response. Instead, it quietly redirects and returns the most recent trading session's data with the previous date stamped in MM/DD/YYYY format. Naive scrapers mistake this for valid data, polluting backtests with duplicate trading days. AuraSpread explicitly validates that ResponseDate == RequestedDate, logging and discarding any redirected records.
3. Look-Ahead Bias Prevention in Signal Generation ▼
Statistical arbitrage backtests often cheat by computing standard deviations or medians over the entire multi-year dataset ("full-sample z-score"). AuraSpread uses an expanding median and Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) that uses data strictly from day 1..t. Furthermore, signals generated at the close of day t are executed only at the close of day t+1, preventing unrealistic same-bar execution.
4. Execution Microstructure: Settlement ≠ Fill & Volume ≠ Depth ▼
Settlement Price is not an executable price: Daily Bhavcopy reports the official Daily Settlement Price (DSP), which is a volume-weighted average of the last 30 minutes. In thin contracts like GOLDPETAL and GOLDGUINEA, wide bid-ask spreads (₹15–₹40/10g) mean you cannot trade two legs at settlement prices simultaneously.

Volume is not Depth: A day with 5,000 lots of volume might only have 2 lots sitting on the top-of-book at any given second. Slicing orders across illiquid retail contracts causes immediate market impact.
5. GOLDTEN History Confidence Warning ▼
MCX GOLDTEN was introduced in early 2025. Historical backtest comparisons involving GOLDTEN before mid-2025 have low statistical confidence due to early listing liquidity ramps and limited sample sizes. All GOLDTEN comparisons in AuraSpread are marked with lower confidence tags.
